1mThe hard cases — Year 9 with nothing to score, and "not competitive anywhere yet"

Year 9 · NSW
There's nothing to score yet — and that's the good news
You won't have an ATAR for three years and you can't sit the UCAT until Year 12. Anyone showing you a chance percentage today is guessing. What we can do is make sure nothing closes off before you get there.
Your band unlocks in Year 11
As soon as your school gives you a predicted ATAR, this fills in.
What actually matters now
Keep Chemistry and Maths open for Year 11
Two of the six NSW-eligible programs list Chemistry as a prerequisite. Dropping it in Year 10 is the one decision at your age that removes options.
Decide by: subject selection, Term 3 Year 10
Check whether you're in a rural catchment
Rural pathways need five years of residence. Knowing now means the clock is already running, rather than something you discover too late.
Five minutes, once
Read what one program actually asks for
Pick a university and read its real rules. Understanding how selection works is worth more at your age than any practice test.
Suggested: UNSW · 6 min read
One thing not to do yet
Don't start UCAT coaching. Practice scores at Year 9 predict almost nothing about Year 12, and it's three years of pressure for no measurable gain.

No direct-entry program is a realistic chance this year
We'd rather tell you plainly than let you spend a year on preferences that can't land. This is about undergraduate direct entry for 2027 — it is not a verdict on medicine, and three routes stay fully open.

Three routes still open
Graduate entry via a science degree
Most common
Your ATAR clears Biomedical Science at four Victorian universities. Graduate medicine then selects on your degree GPA and the GAMSAT — a completely fresh set of numbers, three years from now.
Rural and regional pathways
If you have rural residence, two programs drop their threshold below your current ATAR. This is the one thing that could change your 2027 answer, and it takes five minutes to check.
Reapply next year as a non-school leaver
You can sit the UCAT again and apply without a new ATAR. Your 2410 was your first sitting; second sittings improve by about 150 points on average.
What we won't tell you
That another 40 UCAT points would fix this. At your current ATAR the academic thresholds are the binding constraint at every direct-entry program, so UCAT coaching this year would not change the answer. That's worth knowing before anyone sells it to you.

Common questions
Why one-off rather than a subscription?
Criteria change once a year, so a monthly charge would bill you for months where nothing moved. Complete covers one intake year, updates included.
Is a Year 9 result worth paying for?
